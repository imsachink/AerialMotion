Architecture & Features

Engineered natively for Apple Silicon.
Zero compromises.

Traditional live wallpaper apps run continuous video players on top of your desktop, causing fan noise, high memory pressure, and broken lock screens. AerialMotion solves this at the system level.

Lock Screen Synchronized

Your video loops smoothly on your desktop AND lock screen. When waking, sleeping, or unlocking, macOS transitions seamlessly without glitching.

Wallspace Battery Guard

Intelligently throttles and pauses when your MacBook is on battery, in Low Power Mode, or when full-screen apps hide your desktop.

Native Hardware Remuxing

Utilizes Apple Media Engine hardware decoders to convert standard MP4/MOV videos into macOS-native aerial containers in seconds.

Native Centered Settings

Settings window opens cleanly in the center of your screen with dedicated macOS AppKit controls that never accidentally collapse.

1-Click GitHub Auto-Updates

Stay current automatically. AerialMotion integrates with GitHub Releases to notify you and upgrade with zero hassle.

100% Reversible & Safe

Never overwrites protected system files. Revert to Apple’s standard dynamic aerials anytime with a single click in the menu bar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Answers to common questions about setting custom live wallpapers on macOS Sonoma and Sequoia, performance, battery life, and formats.

AerialMotion requires macOS Sonoma (14.0+) or macOS Sequoia (15.0+), running natively on both Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and Intel-based Macs. These macOS releases introduced Apple's modern dynamic Aerial screensaver architecture that AerialMotion integrates into.
No! Traditional live wallpaper apps decode video in continuous software loops that consume 15%–35% CPU. AerialMotion uses Apple's native hardware media engine to remux your video directly into the native WallpaperAgent container. It consumes ~0% idle CPU and automatically pauses playback when your MacBook is on battery or in Low Power Mode.
AerialMotion supports standard .mp4 and .mov video files across 1080p, 4K, and 8K resolutions at up to 60fps and 120fps (ProMotion). It natively processes HEVC (H.265), H.264, and Apple ProRes codecs without generational compression loss.
AerialMotion hooks directly into macOS WallpaperAgent's hierarchical aerial container format, generating a unified dual-layer package. When you lock, sleep, or wake your Mac, macOS smoothly transitions the video from screensaver to static lock and live desktop motion without glitching or restarts.
Yes! Multi-display configurations on macOS Sonoma and Sequoia are fully supported, including Apple Studio Display, Pro Display XDR, and third-party ultrawide monitors. Playback remains synchronized across all active displays.
Traditional wallpaper tools run heavy background player daemons that cannot modify or sync the macOS lock screen. The famous Aerial screensaver only activates when your Mac goes idle as a screensaver. AerialMotion is the only lightweight, open-source tool designed to set custom videos as the active live desktop AND lock screen wallpaper simultaneously with zero extra daemons.
AerialMotion is independent, 100% free open-source software built by the community without an expensive Apple Developer commercial certificate. On first open, simply Right-Click (Control-Click) AerialMotion.app and click Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/AerialMotion.app in Terminal to clear Apple's quarantine flag.
Yes. AerialMotion respects macOS window occlusion notifications. Whenever a game, video player, or productivity app runs full-screen or completely occludes the desktop, rendering cycles pause immediately so 100% of GPU and CPU power is dedicated to your active task.
Yes! AerialMotion is 100% non-destructive and reversible. Select "Restore Apple Defaults" from the menu bar settings at any time, or pick any standard wallpaper in macOS System Settings.
Because AerialMotion installs no root daemons, kernel extensions, or hidden LaunchAgents, uninstalling is completely clean: simply quit AerialMotion from the menu bar and drag AerialMotion.app from your Applications folder to the Trash.
